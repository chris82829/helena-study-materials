Question ID: usefulness_suggestions
Source column: v14_nutzen_vorschlaege

German wording as administered (final questionnaire version, wording of 2026-09-23):
Das System hat aus dem verfügbaren Angebot die bestmöglichen Vorschläge gemacht.

English translation (as reported in the paper):
The system made the best possible suggestions from the available offers.

Source framework: ResQue
Source item: ResQue, Table 2, Perceived Usefulness, item 3
Adaptation: German translation adapted to job search; harmonized 7-point scale. The wording refers to the available offers in order to separate the assessment of the advice from the size of the job corpus; it is therefore not a verbatim translation of the original item.
