Question ID: trust_credibility
Source column: v14_vertrauen_glaubwuerdigkeit

German wording as administered (final questionnaire version, wording of 2026-09-23):
Die Einschätzungen des Systems zur Passung der gefundenen Stellen erschienen mir glaubwürdig.

English translation (as reported in the paper):
The system's assessments of how well the retrieved positions fit me seemed credible.

Source framework: ResQue / CRS-Que (based on)
Source item: Based on ResQue Table 2 (Confidence & Trust) and CRS-Que Table 4 (Trust & Confidence)
Adaptation: Two-item adaptation based on the trust constructs of ResQue and CRS-Que; not a complete or validated original scale. The wording refers to the available offers in order to separate the assessment of the advice from the size of the job corpus; it is therefore not a verbatim translation of the original item.
Reference: https://doi.org/10.1145/2043932.2043962 ; https://doi.org/10.1145/3631534
