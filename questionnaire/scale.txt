Agreement scale for all 20 rating items: 1 = strongly disagree; 7 = strongly agree.
German labels: 1 Stimme überhaupt nicht zu; 2 Stimme nicht zu; 3 Stimme eher nicht zu;
4 Weder noch; 5 Stimme eher zu; 6 Stimme zu; 7 Stimme voll und ganz zu.
Additional option: Kann ich nicht beurteilen (cannot judge).

Missing values: All rating items were mandatory but offered the option "cannot judge",
which was stored as an empty cell. MISSING in a rating therefore means "cannot judge".
Experience codes: 0 never; 1 less than weekly; 2 at least weekly but not daily;
3 daily. MISSING ("no answer") remains distinct from 0.
Condition codes: A baseline; B fixed advisor; C configurable advisor.
Construct scores (usefulness, control, transparency: 3 items each; trust: 2 items)
are means computed only when all items of the construct have a valid rating.
The trust pair is an adaptation, not a validated full original scale.
ResQue originally used five scale points; the study uses seven for all ratings.
